\chapter[Independent Benchmark Review Protocol]{Independent Benchmark Review\\Protocol}
\label{app:review-protocol}

The independent review covers 33 unique question records: all 22 audit exceptions, all three hybrid top-20 residuals, and ten deterministic unchanged controls stratified by source class and partition.

For each record, the reviewer assesses:

\begin{itemize}
  \item whether the question is clear and unambiguous;
  \item whether every material answer claim is supported;
  \item whether the canonical citation is correct;
  \item whether the selected evidence is complete;
  \item whether alternative one-child or multi-child evidence routes should be accepted;
  \item whether the question, answer, citation, parent, or evidence identifiers require correction;
  \item whether the record should be excluded or marked uncertain.
\end{itemize}

The reviewer records a name or role and an ISO-format review date. The freeze process rejects missing rows, duplicated identifiers, unknown evidence, incomplete decision fields, and mismatched source hashes. Reviewed exceptions and controls are distinguished from unselected candidate records; the report does not imply that every benchmark row received expert legal review.
